Privacy Policy

Trivia Plus

Last updated: March 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trivia Plus ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application ("App"). By downloading or using Trivia Plus, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We and our third-party partners collect the following types of information:

Information stored on your device

Trivia Plus saves your game progress, wallet balance (coins and XP), profile settings, preferences, and achievement data locally on your device using iOS system storage (UserDefaults). This data may also be synced to our cloud services as described below.

Account and profile data (Firebase Authentication and Firestore)

When you launch the App, we create an account using Firebase Authentication (Google LLC). If you sign into Apple Game Center, this account may be linked to your Game Center identity.

We store the following in Firebase Firestore:

  • Display name, avatar, and profile customization
  • Player level, XP, and progression data
  • Gameplay statistics and achievements
  • Profile visibility settings (public or private)
  • Game Center player ID (for leaderboard linking)
  • Account creation date

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases. All transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information.

Apple may provide us with limited transaction data (such as purchase confirmation) to unlock purchased content.

Usage and analytics data (Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used. This may include:

  • App interactions and events
  • Session duration
  • Device type and OS version
  • General location (country/city level)

Firebase may use a unique identifier associated with your device or installation. This does not directly identify you.

Crash and diagnostics data (Firebase Crashlytics)

Crash reports and diagnostic data are collected to improve stability and performance.

Game Center data

If you use Game Center, your alias and scores may appear on leaderboards.

Trivia question data

Trivia content is fetched from The Trivia API. Requests may include your IP address.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the App
  • Save and restore progress
  • Display leaderboards
  • Improve gameplay and features
  • Analyze usage trends
  • Fix bugs and crashes

We may share limited information with third-party providers (such as Firebase and Apple) to operate the App. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase
  • Apple Game Center
  • The Trivia API

4. Data Retention

Data is retained as long as necessary to provide the App or until you request deletion.

5. International Data Transfers

Data may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Request data deletion
  • Set your profile to private
  • Contact us to opt out of analytics

7. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Not intended for children under 13.
