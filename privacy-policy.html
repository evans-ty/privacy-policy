Privacy Policy

Trivia Plus

Last updated: April 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trivia Plus ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application ("App"). By downloading or using Trivia Plus, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We and our third-party partners collect the following types of information:

Information stored on your device

Trivia Plus stores game progress, coins, XP, preferences, and achievements on your device. This data may also be synced to our cloud services.

Account and profile data (Firebase)

When you use the App, we create an account using Firebase Authentication (Google LLC). This account may be linked to your Apple Game Center identity.

  • Display name, avatar, and profile settings
  • Level, XP, achievements, and gameplay stats
  • Profile visibility settings
  • Game Center player ID
  • Account creation date

Usage and analytics data (Firebase Analytics)

We collect anonymous usage data such as gameplay events, session duration, device type, OS version, and general location (country or city level).

Crash and diagnostics data (Firebase Crashlytics)

We collect crash reports and diagnostics to improve app performance and stability.

Advertising data (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements using Google AdMob. Ads may use limited information such as device identifiers and general location to deliver and measure ad performance.

Trivia Plus does not use App Tracking Transparency (ATT) for cross-app tracking in this version of the App.

Game Center data

If you use Apple Game Center, your alias, scores, and achievements may be visible to other players through leaderboards or multiplayer features.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not store payment information.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain the App
  • Save and restore progress
  • Enable leaderboards and multiplayer features
  • Improve gameplay and performance
  • Analyze usage trends
  • Display advertisements

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase
  • Google AdMob
  • Apple Game Center

4. Data Retention

We retain data as long as necessary to provide the App or until you delete your data.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • You can delete your data at any time using the "Delete My Data" option in Settings
  • You can set your profile to private
  • You may stop using the App to limit data collection

6. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy
