Privacy Policy

Trivia Plus

Last updated: March 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trivia Plus ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application ("App"). By downloading or using Trivia Plus, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We and our third-party partners collect the following types of information:

Information stored on your device

Trivia Plus saves your game progress, wallet balance (coins and XP), profile settings, preferences, and achievement data locally on your device using iOS system storage (UserDefaults). This data may also be synced to our cloud services as described below.

Account and profile data (Firebase Authentication and Firestore)

When you launch the App, we automatically create an anonymous account using Firebase Authentication, provided by Google LLC. If you sign into Apple Game Center, this anonymous account is linked to your Game Center identity, creating a persistent account that can be restored if you reinstall the App or switch devices.

We store the following profile data in Firebase Firestore, a cloud database provided by Google LLC, associated with your account:

  • Display name (chosen by you during onboarding, or a randomly generated placeholder)
  • Avatar and profile border selection
  • Player level and XP total
  • Lifetime games played, perfect games count, correct answers, and best streak
  • Top category and mastery tier
  • Number of achievements unlocked and Adventure Mode worlds completed
  • Whether your profile is set to public or private
  • Whether you have an active premium subscription
  • Your Apple Game Center player ID (used to link your in-app profile to leaderboard entries)
  • The date your profile was first created

This data is used to display your profile on in-app leaderboards and to restore your progress if you reinstall the App. If you set your profile to private, your statistics will not be visible to other players on the leaderboard, though your display name and avatar will still appear alongside your score.

Question reports

If you use the in-game question reporting feature, we collect and store the reported question content, the reason for the report, and a timestamp in Firebase Firestore. No personally identifiable information is attached to these reports.

Usage and analytics data (Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics, provided by Google LLC, to understand how players interact with the App. Firebase Analytics may automatically collect:

  • App events (e.g., screens visited, game rounds completed, daily challenges started)
  • Session duration and engagement metrics
  • Device type, operating system version, and language
  • General geographic region (country or city level)
  • An anonymous device identifier

This data is aggregated and used only to improve the App experience. Firebase Analytics does not collect your name, email address, or precise location.

Crash and diagnostics data (Firebase Crashlytics)

We use Firebase Crashlytics to detect and diagnose app crashes and errors. Crashlytics may collect:

  • Stack traces and crash logs
  • Device state at the time of a crash
  • A randomly generated installation UUID (not linked to your identity)

This data is used solely to fix bugs and improve stability.

App integrity data (Firebase App Check)

We use Firebase App Check with Apple's DeviceCheck framework to verify that requests to our backend services originate from a legitimate, unmodified installation of the App. This process does not collect any personally identifiable information.

Game Center data

If you are signed into Apple Game Center, your Game Center alias and scores are submitted to in-app leaderboards. Your Game Center player ID is also stored in our Firestore database so that we can display your in-app profile name and avatar alongside your leaderboard score. Game Center account data is otherwise governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Trivia question data

The App fetches trivia questions from third-party question APIs. These requests do not include any personally identifiable information beyond a standard IP address assigned by your internet service provider.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your in-app player profile
  • To display your profile and scores on in-app leaderboards
  • To restore your progress when you reinstall the App or switch devices
  • To operate and improve the App and its features
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and technical issues
  • To understand general usage patterns and player engagement
  • To verify the integrity of App requests and protect against abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Trivia Plus integrates the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Firebase Firestore — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Firebase Analytics — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Firebase App Check — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple Game Center — apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Trivia question APIs — used to fetch quiz questions; no personally identifiable information is sent in these requests beyond a standard IP address.

4. Data Retention

Game data stored locally on your device is retained until you delete the App or clear its data. Profile data stored in Firebase Firestore is retained until you request deletion (see Section 6). Analytics and crash data retained by Google Firebase is subject to Google's data retention policies, typically 14 months for Analytics data.

5. Children's Privacy

Trivia Plus is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below and we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Delete your data: You can request deletion of your Firestore profile and all associated cloud data at any time using the "Delete My Data" option in the App's Settings. This will permanently remove your profile from our servers. Locally stored data can be removed by deleting the App from your device.
  • Make your profile private: You can set your profile to private in Settings at any time. This hides your statistics from other players on the leaderboard.
  • Opt out of Firebase Analytics: Contact us at the email below and we can disable analytics collection for your installation.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Locally stored game data is protected by iOS's built-in security. Cloud data stored in Firebase Firestore is protected by Firebase security rules and Firebase App Check, which restricts access to authenticated App installations only. However, no method of data storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
